🕹️ The Arcade

Browser games from this suite's own workshop — every card links to a live, playable GitHub Pages deployment (verified 2026-07-30). No installs, no accounts; the games open in a new tab and run entirely in your browser.

Bathhouse Brigade

Desktop · keyboardfalling-block puzzle

A retro Soviet-constructivist falling-block comedy: each of the seven pieces is an enormous grumpy old man in a Speedo posed as the block silhouette. Clear four courses at once for a FULL BRIGADE bonus.

Vanilla HTML/JS, no build step · arrows move, Z/X rotate, Space drops, P pause, M mute

The phone-and-tablet edition of Bathhouse Brigade: the same seven comrades with 44px+ on-screen keys and safe gestures — no swipe can accidentally hard-drop your shift. The full desktop keyboard game is preserved.

Vanilla HTML/JS, no build step · portrait and landscape, touch keys + gestures

Chromatic Chains — Desktop Edition

Desktop · mouse + keyboardchain-drawing puzzle

An original puzzle: as the Weaver, draw chains through touching same-colour glass beads and close a loop to surge the board. A 44-frame campaign in five chapters introduces ten mechanics one at a time, with medals and saved progression.

Vanilla JS ES modules, canvas, zero dependencies · needs a window of at least 1024×640

Play in your browser ↗ Source on GitHub ↗

Chromatic Chains — Mobile Edition

Mobile · touch-firstchain-drawing puzzle

The touch-first edition of Chromatic Chains: drag to weave, tap to step, backtrack to undo, with all 44 frames carried over byte-for-byte and controls kept outside the playfield. Full keyboard play is retained.

Vanilla JS ES modules, canvas, zero dependencies · portrait and landscape

DOOM (1993) — Shareware

The unmodified DOOM v1.9 shareware episode running in-browser through the EmulatorJS PrBoom core. Mobile gets a start button and labeled touch controls; desktop uses the classic keys.

DOOM © id Software — an unaffiliated shareware deployment, not open source · arrows move, Ctrl fires, Alt strafes, Space uses, Esc menu
