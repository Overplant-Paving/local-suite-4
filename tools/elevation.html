Elevation Profiler

Look up the elevation of any point, and draw the terrain profile between two points — handy for hikes, ridgelines, line-of-sight, and drainage. US points use the USGS; anywhere else falls back to open elevation data.

Points

Enter coordinates in decimal degrees. Point A alone gives a single elevation; both points draw a profile.

Point A (start)
Point B (end) — optional

Data: USGS EPQS (epqs.nationalmap.gov, US, 1/3-arc-second) with Open-Elevation (api.open-elevation.com, global) fallback. Lookups are cached on your machine. No account, no tracking.
