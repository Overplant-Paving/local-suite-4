Election & Voting Info

Official election-office links for all 50 states and D.C., national voting resources, and a countdown to the next federal election. This is a curated directory — always confirm dates and rules with your own state's election office.

Registration deadlines vary. Most states cut off voter registration 15–30 days before an election; a number of states offer same-day or Election-Day registration, and North Dakota has no registration at all. Deadlines and rules change every cycle — treat the guidance here as a starting point and verify with your state's official office below.
