Geocoder & Coordinate Toolbox

Turn an address into coordinates and back, convert between decimal and degrees-minutes-seconds, and measure distance and bearing between two points.

Address → coordinates (forward geocode)

Open-Meteo is best for cities and landmarks; Census resolves full US street addresses; Nominatim handles both (1 request/second).

Coordinates → address (reverse geocode)

Uses Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). Also accepts a single "lat, lon" pasted from anywhere.

Decimal ↔ degrees-minutes-seconds

Distance & bearing between two points

Great-circle (haversine) distance and the initial compass bearing from A to B.
Data: Open-Meteo geocoding · US Census geocoder (JSONP) · Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (identified by email, 1 req/s). Coordinate math runs locally.
