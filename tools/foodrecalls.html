Food Recall Alerts

FDA food recalls that reach your state — including nationwide ones — newest first, color-coded by how serious the hazard is.

Class I — reasonable chance of serious harm or death. Class II — temporary or reversible harm. Class III — unlikely to cause harm. "Nationwide" recalls are included for your state.

Data: openFDA · api.fda.gov/food/enforcement — FDA recall enforcement reports. Cached locally, no account.
